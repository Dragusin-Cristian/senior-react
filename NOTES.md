# NOTES FOR EACH SECTION

## Design Systems: Core Concepts

- Why do we need them?
    - Accessability
    - Consistency
    - Frequent updates
    - Onboarding
    - Speed

- Downsides:
    - Time
    - Evolution
    - Maintenance
    - Investment

- Team Structures
    - Centralized Model: a core single team responsible for the design => speed
![centralized team model](<SCREENSHOTS/Screenshot 2025-06-14 at 18.13.45.png>)
    - Distributed Model: consumers are invited to participate => innovation
![distributed team Model](<SCREENSHOTS/Screenshot 2025-06-14 at 18.16.45.png>)
    - Hybrid Model: both => speed and innovation
![hybrid team Model](<SCREENSHOTS/Screenshot 2025-06-14 at 18.18.20.png>)

- How to avoid mistakes:
    - Don't start for scale. Deliver something first, then scale it.
    - Design and develop some components first, then imply their opinions in the process. 
    - Setup a clear workflow, procedures for collaboration.
    - Document your decisions.
